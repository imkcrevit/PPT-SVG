# Internal Skill: Pangu White / 盘古之白

Create a restrained white-background presentation visual in the "盘古之白" style, with strong Chinese text design.

Visual style:
- Use a clean white or near-white canvas; avoid dark, saturated, gradient, neon, or decorative backgrounds.
- Use thin neutral dividers, quiet gray fills, and one restrained accent color only when it clarifies hierarchy.
- Remove decorative clutter: no corner ornaments, standalone icons, badges, side symbols, glows, or visual filler.
- Prefer editorial structure: clear title, compact modules, precise alignment, generous whitespace, and calm rhythm.

Layout:
- Choose the clearest structure for the request: framework, process, comparison, hierarchy, matrix, timeline, or architecture.
- Keep the composition slide-ready and easy to scan from left to right or top to bottom.
- Use consistent margins, aligned edges, and repeated spacing.
- Avoid overlapping elements and avoid placing text too close to borders or connectors.

Text design:
- Text is the primary design object. Build a clear hierarchy: title, section labels, body labels, and small notes.
- Keep every visible text label concise. Prefer short Chinese phrases for `zh` and short English phrases for `en`.
- For `zh`, avoid English letters unless the user explicitly requires a brand name, API name, product code, or technical acronym.
- For `zh`, do not insert half-width spaces between Chinese characters or Chinese words. Use natural Chinese phrasing without artificial word gaps.
- For `zh`, prefer Chinese punctuation and Chinese labels; avoid decorative English abbreviations, stray Latin letters, and mixed-language filler.
- Center text vertically and horizontally inside cards, blocks, pills, and labels unless the layout clearly needs left alignment.
- For left-aligned text blocks, keep a consistent x position, line height, and padding.
- Do not let text overflow its parent shape. Use fewer words, smaller supported font sizes, wider shapes, or line breaks.
- Avoid dense paragraphs. Use short labels and compact supporting notes.

Good uses:
- executive summaries
- strategy frameworks
- capability maps
- product or AI feature explanations
- white-paper style PPT diagrams

Global requirements:
- Follow the active output language exactly: `zh` outputs Simplified Chinese; `en` outputs English.
- Keep the final result calm, white, textual, and business-grade, matching the "盘古之白" style.
- Do not add decorative side symbols, edge icons, corner marks, ornamental badges, or standalone symbols.
- Ensure all text fits cleanly within the SVG canvas and within its intended visual container.
